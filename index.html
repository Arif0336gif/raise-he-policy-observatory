Respondent-level model

A multi-domain alignment model.

The study tests associations among respondent-level domains. Institutional clustering is treated as a design and inference feature—not as evidence for institution-level effects.

Each case used 30 β=.12 and 20 null replications: 150 total. Stress-case downstream denominators include only testable estimands.

Maximum observed Monte Carlo SE: 11.5 percentage points for power and 12.5 points for Type-I error; estimates are therefore deliberately presented as feasibility evidence, not promises.

Base-case decision

A target near 1,000 is defensible for an interaction around β=.12 under expected measurement and clustering conditions.

Stress-case boundary

Weak loadings, 10% missingness and ICC .08 reduce convergence and power; they trigger a simpler model, narrower claims or additional recruitment.

Invariance rule

No latent context means will be reported without defensible equivalence. Partial invariance or context-specific modules will be documented when needed.

Method and estimand details

The exact audit fitted configural, metric and scalar conditions with robust WLSMV. The primary H2 estimand is the capability × professional-learning coefficient in a country-fixed-effects regression of within-context-standardised WLSMV factor scores, adjusted for perceived governance, with CR1 institution-clustered inference (df=G−1).

  • Invariance decisions combine robust model comparisons, fit change and prespecified item diagnostics; global tests alone do not determine comparability.
  • If metric comparability fails, slopes remain context-specific. Scalar or defensible partial invariance is required for latent-mean comparison.
  • The audit supports a conditional design decision. Pilot-calibrated thresholds, loadings and missingness will be re-simulated before preregistration.

Responsible interpretation

Designed to diagnose, not judge.

RAISE-HE supports developmental decisions while preserving clear limits on comparison, causality and institutional use.

01

No public scores or rankings

No synthetic or empirical country score, institutional league table or comparative ranking appears on this page.

02

Measurement before comparison

Context means require scalar or defensible partial invariance; unsupported comparisons will not be reported.

03

No causal claims

The principal quantitative model is observational and estimates associations, not intervention effects.

04

No compliance certification

The toolkit cannot certify legal compliance or replace institutional legal, ethics or data-protection review.

05

No punitive use

Outputs are excluded from staff appraisal, employment decisions, disciplinary action and automated decision-making.

06

Human-verified release

Evidence is screened, coded, reconciled and reviewed before any substantive public interpretation is released.
