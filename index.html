Research model & interpretation safeguards

Aligned to MSCA-PF Part B1 · values remain interface fixtures

Central proposition Faculty capability + professional-learning quality + perceived governance conditions → self-reported enacted responsible practice
Faculty capability

AI literacy, critical evaluation, pedagogical judgement and confidence using AI-supported teaching tools.

Professional-learning quality

Relevance, accessibility, continuity and integration of staff development into professional practice.

Perceived governance conditions

Faculty-reported policy clarity, leadership, infrastructure, data safeguards and implementation support.

Enacted responsible practice · outcome

Self-reported decisions concerning disclosure, verification, privacy, fairness and human oversight.
