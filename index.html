Base-case decision

A target near 1,000 is conditionally defensible for an interaction around β=.12 under expected measurement and clustering conditions.

Stress-case response

Weak loadings, 10% missingness and ICC .08 trigger a simpler model, narrower claims or additional recruitment.

Invariance gate

No latent context means without scalar or defensible partial invariance. Metric failure keeps slopes context-specific.

Method, estimand and interpretation details

The exact audit fitted configural, metric and scalar conditions with robust WLSMV. The primary H2 estimand is the capability × professional-learning coefficient in a country-fixed-effects regression of within-context-standardised WLSMV factor scores, adjusted for perceived governance, with CR1 institution-clustered inference (df=G−1).

  • Invariance decisions combine robust model comparisons, fit change and prespecified item diagnostics; global tests alone do not determine comparability.
  • If metric comparability fails, slopes remain context-specific. Scalar or defensible partial invariance is required for latent-mean comparison.
  • Pilot-calibrated thresholds, loadings and missingness will be re-simulated before preregistration.

Responsible interpretation

Designed to diagnose, not judge.

RAISE-HE supports developmental decisions while preserving strict limits on comparison, causality and institutional use.

01

No public scores or rankings

No synthetic or empirical country score, institutional league table or comparative ranking appears here.

02

Measurement before comparison

Context means require scalar or defensible partial invariance; unsupported comparisons will not be reported.

03

No causal claims

The principal quantitative model is observational and estimates associations, not intervention effects.

04

No compliance certification

The toolkit cannot certify legal compliance or replace legal, ethics or data-protection review.

05

No punitive use

Outputs are excluded from staff appraisal, employment decisions, disciplinary action and automated decision-making.

06

Human-verified release

Evidence is screened, coded, reconciled and reviewed before substantive public interpretation is released.
